GymQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

GymQuest ("the app") is a personal fitness-tracking app. This page explains what data the app accesses, what it collects, and what it does with it. We built GymQuest to work fully offline by default — nothing about your workouts is sent anywhere unless you explicitly turn on an optional feature.

Core workout tracking (no account required)

Logging workouts, tracking XP and character progression, browsing the exercise library, and viewing your workout history all work without creating an account and without any internet connection. This data is stored in a local database on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Health Connect (optional)

If you choose to connect Android Health Connect, GymQuest requests read-only access to your exercise session data so it can check whether a workout you logged matches real activity data from your other fitness apps or wearables, and mark it with a "Verified" badge. This check happens entirely on your device. GymQuest never uploads, stores off-device, or shares any Health Connect data — it is read, compared, and discarded in the same session. You can revoke this access at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions.

Community (optional, requires Google sign-in)

If you choose to sign in with Google to use the Community tab, GymQuest collects:

  • Name, email address, and profile photo link from your Google account, used to identify you within the app and via Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you choose to post — text posts, comments, routine notes, and music links — stored in Cloud Firestore and visible to other GymQuest users, since sharing that content with the community is the entire purpose of the feature.

This data is processed and stored by Firebase (a Google service acting on our behalf), transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell this data or share it with any other third party. If you never open the Community tab or never sign in, none of this applies to you.

What we don't collect

GymQuest has no advertising SDK and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect your location, financial information, private messages, photos/videos (the profile photo is stored as a link to your existing Google account photo, not an uploaded image), web browsing activity, or device identifiers beyond what's needed for Firebase Authentication to function.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your Community account data (name, email, posts, and comments associated with your account), email us at aleboytron11@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, and we will delete it within a reasonable time. Deleting the app from your device immediately removes all locally stored workout data, which we never had a copy of in the first place.

Children's privacy

GymQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
